# UC2 Memo Review

## Verdict and Overall Assessment
I reviewed `outputs/UC2/feasibility_memo.md` against `outputs/UC2/discovery_results.json`, the source records, and the trace. I agree with the overall assessment that **must need N2 is currently unmet** because the `product_codes_2024` wiki notes no mapping table exists for retired pre-2024 `"L"` codes, `internal.sales.products` lists current codes only without effective dates or unique names, and the `2024-02-05` to `2024-03-10` till migration gap must be treated as missing rather than zero.

## Per-Source Verdicts and Observations
- `internal.sales.daily_by_product` (`feasible_with_conditions` in memo): Serves `N1` across `history` and `recent` on paper, but access is confidential/untested, net vs. gross and cut-off timezone are undocumented, the `2024-02-05`–`2024-03-10` window is a missing-data gap, and it cannot resolve `N2` without a code mapping.
- `internal.sales.products` (`feasible_with_conditions` in memo): Useful only as a current product-name reference (64 rows, refreshed `2025-03-31`); does not provide retired `"L"` codes or version history for `N2`, and name matching has ambiguity risk.
- `api.open_meteo_archive` (`feasible_with_conditions` in memo): Returns `temperature_2m_max` across the probed `history` and `recent` dates, but shop coordinates are undocumented (probe defaulted to `45.46, 9.19` -> `45.448154, 9.169279`), upstream licensing is mixed (`C3S`, `DWD`, `UK Met Office`) with unknown commercial use, and attribution (`Weather data by Open-Meteo.com` + link) is required.
- `api.nager_public_holidays` (`feasible_with_conditions` in memo): After the G7 profiler fix, all four target years (`2023`, `2024`, `2025`, `2026`) were probed and returned HTTP 200 (13 records in 2023–2025, 14 in 2026), moving `N4` from `partially_met` to `met_with_conditions`. However, `LIC-UNKNOWN` remains a production blocker, regional/patron-saint holidays are not covered (`COV-GEO-MISMATCH`), and Easter dates plus the 14th 2026 record are not verified against an official Italian source.
- `internal.store.calendar` (`feasible_with_conditions` in memo): Covers `N5` (`should`) on paper, but was not sampled, has `is_open` / outage-day rules undocumented, and shows a row-count vs. date-span discrepancy (~1,500 rows vs. ~1,744 days from `2022-01-01` to `2026-10-10`).

## Agreements, Overrides, and Gate Decision
- **Agreements:** I agree that `N2` is unmet without a retired-to-current code mapping (or agreed name-matching rule), that `2024-02-05` to `2024-03-10` sales rows must be treated as missing, and that external/internal governance conditions (`LIC-UNKNOWN` on Nager holidays, mixed licensing/location on Open-Meteo, and access/schema checks on internal tables) must be addressed.
- **Overrides:** I overrule treating `api.nager_public_holidays` as `feasible_with_conditions` for production while `LIC-UNKNOWN` remains an unresolved blocker and Easter/2026 holiday dates have not been verified against an official Italian source; `N4` should be held or switched to an official Italian calendar until cleared.
- **Gate Decision:** Stop / hold progression on product-level history analysis (`stop`) until Retail Operations confirms whether a code mapping exists and whether a name-based match is acceptable, alongside resolving the access, calendar, and licensing conditions.
